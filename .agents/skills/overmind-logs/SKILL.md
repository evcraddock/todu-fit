---
name: overmind-logs
description: Inspect the local Automerge sync server logs under Overmind.
---

Use `make status` to inspect service state and `make logs` to stream output (Ctrl+C to stop following). Use `make connect-sync` for the sync pane. In tmux use Ctrl+b [ to enter scrollback, q to exit scrollback and Ctrl+b d to detach. Do not start, stop or restart the server.
